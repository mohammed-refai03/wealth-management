Our Heritage

Designed for Families Who Value Financial Security and Confidence

Stackly was founded on a singular conviction: the traditional wealth management industry was fundamentally misaligned with client interests. For decades, conventional brokerage houses and commercial banks pushed proprietary, high-commission financial products.

We rejected this model entirely. As an independent, fee-only Registered Investment Advisor (RIA), Stackly operates strictly under the legal standard of fiduciary obligation—legally and morally bound to place our clients' financial well-being above all else. Today, we oversee more than $450 Million in private capital, family dynasty trusts, and corporate balance-sheet reserves.

Covenant I: The 100% Fee-Only Doctrine

Zero Commission

Every dollar earned by Stackly is directly invoiced to the client as an agreed percentage of assets under management. We refuse 100% of 12b-1 mutual fund trails, broker-dealer soft dollars, and placement bounties.

Covenant II: Non-Proprietary Architecture

Open Universe

Stackly does not manufacture, sponsor, or issue internal financial products. Our investment committee scans the global universe of 35,000+ institutional vehicles solely on risk-adjusted merits without in-house bias.

Covenant III: Institutional Segregated Custody

SIPC Insured
