Unlike traditional brokerage houses and retail commercial banks that sell proprietary investment products or accept manager commissions, Stackly is a 100% Fee-Only Registered Investment Advisor (RIA). Under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940, we operate under a strict legal fiduciary mandate—obligating us to place your financial interests above our own at all times with absolute fee transparency.

Stackly enforces complete separation between investment advice and custody. All client capital is held in segregated accounts under your own legal name at top-tier institutional custodians including Charles Schwab & Co. and Fidelity Institutional. Your assets are safeguarded by SIPC coverage up to $500,000 (including $250,000 for cash claims), backed by supplemental multi-million-dollar private custody insurance.

We operate with a tiered percentage fee based on Assets Under Management (AUM), starting at 0.65% and scaling down to 0.30% for larger institutional allocations. We charge zero trading commissions, zero product markups, and zero lock-in redemption penalties. Our comprehensive wealth advisory typically begins at $1,000,000 in liquid investable assets, with bespoke family office tiers for mandates exceeding $10,000,000.

We execute continuous algorithmic tax-alpha strategies including daily tax-loss harvesting, asset-location optimization across taxable, Roth, and tax-deferred accounts, Section 1202 Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS) stacking, and charitable appreciated asset gifting—consistently delivering an estimated 1.2% to 2.4% in excess annualized net tax alpha.
